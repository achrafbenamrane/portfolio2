# Voice recording script

Read this straight through, at a normal pace, the way you would talk to
someone visiting your site. Nothing to count and nothing to time — every line
the assistant says is a sentence in here, and the splitter finds each one by
locating its words in the transcript.

**Before you start**

- Press **Win**, type **Sound Recorder**. In its settings, set the format to
  **MP3** and the quality to **Best**.
- Quiet room. No music, no fan, no open window.
- Keep the same distance from the microphone the whole way through.
- If you stumble, stop, say that sentence again, and carry on. The better
  attempt wins.

It takes about two minutes.

---

Hi, I am Achraf Benamrane. I am a full stack developer, a designer, and a
network security engineer, based in Annaba, Algeria.

I work in three areas. Web and mobile development. Network and information
security. And user interface design.

People often ask me where I am from. I live in Annaba, in Algeria.

They also ask if I am looking for work. Yes, I am available for work now. The
best way to reach me is by email.

So let me show you around this desktop.

Here are my projects. Web, mobile, security, and design work.

This is my experience. Where I worked, and what I did there.

These are my diplomas and certificates.

Here is how to reach me. Email is best. My phone and my social links are also
here.

There are a few other things down in the dock.

Opening the Quran player. Choose a reader and a surah.

Downloading my CV now.

Opening my GitHub.

Opening my LinkedIn.

Opening settings. You can turn hand control on and off here.

Closing that.

If you are not sure what to try, just ask me for help.

You can ask me to open my work, my experience, my certificates, or my
contacts. You can also ask who I am, or if I am free for work.

Sometimes the microphone does not catch you properly.

Sorry, I did not understand. Try asking about my work, or how to contact me.

And sometimes a question is outside what I know about.

Sorry, I cannot answer that now. But I can show you my work, my experience, or
my contacts.

One last one, and then we are done.

Hi, I am Achraf. Ask me about my work. Or tell me what to open.

---

When you are finished, say so and I will run `npm run voice:split`.
